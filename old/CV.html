Curriculum Vitae

I completed my undergraduate degree at Monash University with two majors; one in physics and one in astrophysics. Between undergraduate and honours I participated in the International Center for Radio Astronomy summer studentship program in Perth, Australia, where I completed a research project with Dr Luca Cortese and Dr Barabara Catinella looking at tracing gas in galaxies using dust. I then went on to complete honours at Monash Unviersity where I received first class honours for the thesis "Thermonuclear Bursts from Accretion Powered Millisecond Pulsars". I am currently undertaking my PhD with Associate Professor Duncan Galloway and Professor Alexander Heger researching accreting neutron star systems. Please download my CV below.
